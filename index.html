About & Background

\ 03\
\
\
\

I am an art and design historian specializing in Central Asia, Iran, and the Steppe. My research revolves around premodern design systems that facilitated cross-cultural communication and the modern lives of ancient objects.

\

My dissertation-turned-first book project, Fashioning Central Eurasia, explores how dress and fashion shaped social interactions across the Silk Roads. My second project, Eurasian Silver, cataloged 3rd- to 13th-century CE silver vessels across vast Eurasian hoards.
